# ML Journey Study Schedule

This is a suggested study schedule for completing the ML Journey course. Adjust based on your pace and availability.

## 📅 40-Week Part-Time Track (7-10 hours/week)

Double the timeline, spending 2 weeks on each week of the intensive track.

### Suggested Weekly Schedule
- **Weekdays**: 1-1.5 hours per day (5-7.5 hours)
- **Weekend**: 2-3 hours one day

## 📅 Self-Paced Track

Complete modules at your own pace. Suggested minimum commitment:
- **3-4 hours/week**: ~1 year to complete
- **5-7 hours/week**: ~6-9 months to complete
- **8-12 hours/week**: ~4-6 months to complete

## 💡 Study Tips

### Daily Routine
1. **Review** (15 min): Review yesterday's learning
2. **Learn** (60-90 min): New concepts and theory
3. **Practice** (30-60 min): Exercises and coding
4. **Reflect** (15 min): Note key takeaways

### Weekly Routine
1. **Monday**: Start new module/week
2. **Tuesday-Thursday**: Core learning
3. **Friday**: Catch up and review
4. **Weekend**: Projects and assignments

### Monthly Routine
1. **Week 1-3**: New material
2. **Week 4**: Review, consolidation, project work
